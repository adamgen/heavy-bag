# Decisions

## Open questions

| ID | Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q-1 | **Weights: how do customers get them without huge shipping costs?** Our own bricks, moulds, locally sourced standard bricks, lifting plates, something else? | Sets compartment size, cost and business model. Exploration in progress (design plan §3.2). |
| Q-4 | **Weight placement** — confirm base tray (laptop bag) and back-panel sleeve (backpack) in prototype tests. | Balance in the hand, comfort when worn. |
| Q-5 | **Price and look** — working direction in [`brand.md`](brand.md); founder to review. | Drives materials and suppliers. |
| Q-6 | **First market and sales channel** (DTC website, pre-order campaign, retail)? | Shipping heavy weights changes the economics. |
| Q-7 | **Brand name** — keep "Heavy Bag" or rename? | Trademark and domain check in progress. |
| Q-10 | **Knowing the total:** how does a user know they're at or under 30 kg with their laptop and contents? | Add-on scale noted for marketing ([`brand.md`](brand.md)); simpler aids (weight chart, printed guide) still worth having. |

## Decision log

| Date | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-29 | Project started with goals: heavy-on-purpose work bag for a daily suitcase carry; weights adjustable, quick to drop, easy to replace, placed at the bottom or side. | Founding brief. |
| 2026-10-29 | Durability is a critical goal. The bag has a durable plastic or light metal frame, uses extremely durable fabric, and is rated to carry 30 kg. | Founder requirement: the bag must survive heavy daily loads. |
| 2026-10-29 | 30 kg is the **total carry load** (bag, laptop, contents and weights) and the weight people should build up to, not just a strength rating. Bricks top out at about 24 kg. Closes Q-3. | Founder decision. |
| 2026-10-29 | Recommended carry: one hand until fatigue, switch hands, keep alternating — about 10 min per side on a 20 min walk. The bag must carry the same in either hand. | Founder decision; defines the core exercise. |
| 2026-10-29 | Product line at launch: a **backpack** and a **laptop bag**, sharing one weight system. Closes Q-2. | Founder decision. |
| 2026-10-29 | Frame: **6061-T6 aluminium load spine + HDPE panels**; moulded glass-filled nylon parts considered later at volume. Closes Q-8. | Delegated to Claude: strong and light, no tooling for prototypes, no plastic creep under constant 30 kg. |
| 2026-10-29 | Fabric: **several outer fabric options on one shared internal structure** — Core (ballistic/Cordura-class nylon) at launch, Tech (sailcloth laminate / UHMWPE) and Heritage (leather / waxed canvas) to follow. Closes Q-9. | Founder asked for a few options; one structure keeps every option equally durable. |
| 2026-10-29 | Add-on scale kept as a **marketing note**, not a core feature. | Founder decision. |
| 2026-10-29 | Brand look and price direction drafted by Claude as interim brand lead ([`brand.md`](brand.md)); founder will review later. | Founder delegated for now. |
