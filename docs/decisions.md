# Decisions

## Current scope (2026-11-08)

Product design only, then POC preparation in Israel or abroad after the open
choices settle. Ads/Kickstarter follow a satisfying product. Legal, market
proof and shipping economics are deferred; US steel shipping is not being
reopened as a design blocker. No orders/outreach are authorized here.

The older entries below are history, not current capacity specifications.
[Tech pack v0.2](tech-pack.md) and revised SVGs supersede v0.1 geometry.

## Decision log

| 2026-10-29 | Durability is a critical goal. The bag has a durable plastic or light metal frame, uses extremely durable fabric, and is rated to carry 30 kg. | Founder requirement: the bag must survive heavy daily loads. |
| 2026-10-29 | 30 kg is the **total carry load** (bag, laptop, contents and weights) **Superseded 2026-11-08:** not a required progression goal; 24 kg is only an example allowance, not a guaranteed brick capacity. Closes Q-3. | Founder decision. |
| 2026-10-29 | Recommended carry: one hand until fatigue, switch hands, keep alternating — about 10 min per side on a 20 min walk. The bag must carry the same in either hand. | Founder decision; defines the core exercise. |
| 2026-10-29 | Product line at launch: a **backpack** and a **laptop bag**, sharing one weight system. Closes Q-2. | Founder decision. |
| 2026-10-29 | Frame: **6061-T6 aluminium load spine + HDPE panels**; moulded glass-filled nylon parts considered later at volume. Closes Q-8. | Delegated to Claude: strong and light, no tooling for prototypes, no plastic creep under constant 30 kg. |
| 2026-10-29 | Fabric: **several outer fabric options on one shared internal structure** — Core (ballistic/Cordura-class nylon) at launch, Tech (sailcloth laminate / UHMWPE) and Heritage (leather / waxed canvas) to follow.
