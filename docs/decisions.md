# Decisions

## Open questions

| ID | Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q-1 | **Bricks: our own standard bricks, real building bricks, or both?** | Sets compartment size, cost, cleanliness and business model (see design plan §3.1). |
| Q-2 | **Bag form:** briefcase, tote, messenger, or a range? | Drives the look, handle design and where weight can go. |
| Q-3 | **Target load range** — 0–10 kg, 0–15 kg, more? | Sets structure, brick count and safety factor. |
| Q-4 | **Bottom vs. side** weight placement? | Leading candidate is bottom; to be settled by prototype tests. |
| Q-5 | **Price point** and positioning (premium leather vs. technical fabric)? | Drives materials and suppliers. |
| Q-6 | **First market and sales channel** (DTC website, pre-order campaign, retail)? | Shipping heavy weights changes the economics. |
| Q-7 | **Brand name** — keep "Heavy Bag" or rename? | Search and trademark clash with boxing bags. |
