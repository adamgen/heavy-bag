# Decisions

## Open questions

| ID | Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q-1 | **Bricks: our own standard bricks, real building bricks, or both?** | Sets compartment size, cost, cleanliness and business model (see design plan §3.1). |
| Q-2 | **Bag form:** briefcase, tote, messenger, or a range? | Drives the look, handle design and where weight can go. |
| Q-4 | **Bottom vs. side** weight placement? | Leading candidate is bottom; to be settled by prototype tests. |
| Q-5 | **Price point** and positioning (premium leather vs. technical fabric)? | Drives materials and suppliers. |
| Q-6 | **First market and sales channel** (DTC website, pre-order campaign, retail)? | Shipping heavy weights changes the economics. |
| Q-7 | **Brand name** — keep "Heavy Bag" or rename? | Search and trademark clash with boxing bags. |
| Q-8 | **Frame material:** aluminium, glass-filled nylon, or a mix? | Weight, cost, tooling and durability (design plan §3.5). Working plan: aluminium for prototypes, revisit for production. |
| Q-9 | **Outer fabric:** ballistic nylon, sailcloth laminate, leather? | Durability, look and price point; tied to Q-5. |
| Q-10 | **Knowing the total:** how does a user know they're at or under 30 kg with their laptop and contents? (Brick-count chart, printed guide, built-in scale?) | 30 kg is the carry target, so users need a simple way to hit it without overshooting. |

## Decision log

| Date | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-29 | Project started with goals: heavy-on-purpose work bag for a daily suitcase carry; weights adjustable, quick to drop, easy to replace, placed at the bottom or side. | Founding brief. |
| 2026-10-29 | Durability is a critical goal. The bag has a durable plastic or light metal frame, uses extremely durable fabric, and is rated to carry 30 kg. | Founder requirement: the bag must survive heavy daily loads. |
| 2026-10-29 | 30 kg is the **total carry load** (bag, laptop, contents and weights) and the weight people should build up to, not just a strength rating. Bricks top out at about 24 kg. Closes Q-3. | Founder decision. |
| 2026-10-29 | Recommended carry: one hand until fatigue, switch hands, keep alternating — about 10 min per side on a 20 min walk. The bag must carry the same in either hand. | Founder decision; defines the core exercise. |
